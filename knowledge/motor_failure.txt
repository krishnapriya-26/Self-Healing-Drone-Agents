Drone Motor Failure Procedure

Motor failure is a critical flight safety condition.

When a motor failure is detected, the drone should not continue a normal inspection mission.

The system should evaluate flight stability and remaining motor capability.

Recommended recovery actions include:
1. Stop the inspection mission.
2. Reduce unnecessary movement.
3. Evaluate flight stability.
4. Initiate Return-To-Home if stable flight is possible.
5. If stable flight is not possible, identify the nearest safe landing area.
6. Request human approval for high-risk recovery actions.

Mission continuation should be blocked when motor failure creates an unsafe flight condition.
