Drone Overheating Procedure

High temperature can indicate motor, battery, processor, or environmental problems.

A temperature above 80 degrees Celsius should be considered a critical overheating condition.

Recommended actions:
1. Reduce mission workload.
2. Stop non-essential processing.
3. Monitor temperature continuously.
4. Reduce altitude or movement when appropriate.
5. Initiate Return-To-Home if temperature continues increasing.
6. Request human intervention if the temperature reaches an unsafe level.

The system should not continue a normal inspection mission during severe overheating.
