Drone Emergency Response Procedures

The autonomous system must prioritize safety over mission completion.

Critical failures include:
- Motor failure
- Extremely low battery
- GPS failure
- Severe overheating
- Loss of communication
- Unstable flight

For critical failures, the system should evaluate available recovery actions.

Possible actions:
- Pause mission
- Return-To-Home
- Reroute mission
- Recapture image
- Assign another drone
- Land at a safe location
- Request human approval

High-risk actions must require human approval.

If an autonomous recovery action cannot be safely determined, the system must use a fallback procedure and request human intervention.
